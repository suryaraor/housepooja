Our Story

Run by Sunil & Ramya, not a call centre

Housepooja is a family-run practice based in Bachupally, Hyderabad. Sunil Kumar Tekumal and Ramya Tekumal coordinate every booking personally — the same two people you speak to on the phone are the ones making sure the priest, the samagri and the caterer all show up on time on the day.
