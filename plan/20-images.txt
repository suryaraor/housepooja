20 image prompts for Housepooja (Gruhapravesh package website)
Style baseline for all prompts: hyper-realistic photography, natural warm lighting, shallow depth of field, shot on a full-frame DSLR with an 85mm or 50mm lens, rich maroon/gold/marigold color palette to match the site, South Indian housewarming (Gruhapravesh) setting, no text or watermarks, no visible faces of children unless described, culturally accurate props and attire.

1. hero-doorway-welcome.jpg
A hyper-realistic photo of a decorated South Indian home entrance during a Gruhapravesh ceremony: a fresh mango-leaf and marigold toran hangs above the open wooden door, a brass kalash with a coconut and mango leaves sits on the threshold beside a hand-drawn rice-flour kolam, warm golden-hour sunlight streaming in from outside, soft bokeh of guests' feet approaching in sandals, shot on 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, editorial real-estate-and-ritual photography style.

2. priest-performing-homam.jpg
A hyper-realistic close-up photograph of an experienced Indian priest (pujari) in a traditional dhoti and angavastram, seated cross-legged beside a small brick homam kunda, pouring ghee from a wooden spoon into bright orange flames, smoke curling upward, his hands mid-gesture, soft golden firelight illuminating his face and gray beard, dark blurred background, shot on 85mm f/1.8, documentary ceremony photography.

3. kalash-samagri-flatlay.jpg
A hyper-realistic overhead flat-lay photograph of Gruhapravesh pooja samagri neatly arranged on a red silk cloth: a brass kalash topped with a coconut and mango leaves, turmeric and kumkum in small silver bowls, betel leaves and areca nuts, a garland of marigold, a small oil lamp (diya), incense sticks, and a coconut, soft diffused studio light from the top left, rich color contrast, shot on 50mm macro lens.

4. mandap-backdrop-styled.jpg
A hyper-realistic wide shot of an elegant pooja mandap backdrop set up for a housewarming ceremony: a wooden four-pillar mandap draped in marigold and jasmine garlands, brass lamps (deepams) on either side, a maroon and gold silk cloth canopy overhead, a low wooden platform with a floral rangoli beneath it, warm ambient indoor lighting, empty of people, architectural interior photography style, 24mm lens.

5. diya-marigold-petals.jpg
A hyper-realistic macro photograph of a single lit clay diya resting on a bed of scattered marigold petals, the flame glowing warm orange against a soft dark background, a thin wisp of smoke rising, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh, shot on 100mm macro lens, still-life ritual photography.

6. flower-garland-closeup.jpg
A hyper-realistic close-up photograph of a craftsman's hands stringing a fresh marigold and jasmine garland, vivid orange and white petals in sharp focus, natural window light from the side, blurred workshop background with baskets of loose flowers, shot on 85mm lens, warm color grading.

7. homam-fire-ritual-wide.jpg
A hyper-realistic wide-angle photograph of a full homam fire ceremony in progress inside a decorated Indian home: the priest and a young couple seated around the fire pit performing ahutis, smoke rising toward a decorated ceiling, family members seated cross-legged nearby watching respectfully, warm firelight mixed with soft daylight, shot on 24-70mm lens, candid documentary style.

8. couple-coconut-threshold.jpg
A hyper-realistic photograph of a young Indian couple in traditional attire (the woman in a silk saree, the man in a kurta) about to break a coconut at their new home's threshold as part of the Gruhapravesh ritual, a brass kalash and rice-flour kolam visible at their feet, soft afternoon light, candid emotional expression, shot on 50mm lens, wedding-style ceremony photography.

9. puja-thali-decorated.jpg
A hyper-realistic overhead photograph of a fully decorated brass pooja thali: kumkum, turmeric, uncooked rice, a small betel-nut, a lit wick lamp, flower petals, and a tiny bell arranged symmetrically, resting on a red cloth, soft diffused light, shot on 50mm macro lens, crisp detail on the brass engravings.

10. satvik-food-banana-leaf.jpg
A hyper-realistic close-up photograph of a traditional South Indian satvik feast served on a fresh banana leaf: rice, sambar, rasam, two vegetable curries, papad, pickle, and a sweet payasam in small bowls, steam faintly rising, warm natural light from a nearby window, shot on 50mm lens, food photography with rich color saturation.

11. serving-staff-in-action.jpg
A hyper-realistic candid photograph of catering staff in white uniforms serving hot rice and sambar to seated guests on banana leaves during a housewarming lunch, motion blur on the ladle, warm indoor lighting, joyful blurred guests in the background, shot on 35mm lens, photojournalistic event photography.

12. cow-puja-gau-puja.jpg
A hyper-realistic photograph of a gentle brown Indian cow decorated with a marigold garland around its neck, standing calmly in front of a home entrance while a family member performs aarti with a small oil lamp, soft late-afternoon light, warm tones, shot on 50mm lens, candid ritual photography.

13. return-gifts-arranged.jpg
A hyper-realistic photograph of small return-gift boxes wrapped in maroon and gold paper with a marigold sprig tucked into the ribbon, arranged in a neat row on a wooden table, soft window light, shallow depth of field, shot on 85mm lens, product-style still life.

14. rangoli-kolam-entrance.jpg
A hyper-realistic overhead photograph of an intricate, colorful rangoli (kolam) drawn with rice flour and colored powders at a home's entrance, geometric floral pattern in maroon, gold, white and green, a brass oil lamp placed at its center, natural daylight, shot on 24mm lens from directly above, sharp fine detail.

15. photographer-candid-moment.jpg

16. brass-bell-and-diya.jpg

18. incense-smoke-detail.jpg
