Worth knowing before you book

Customisation: regional tradition (Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, North Indian), the priest's preferred language, and dietary preferences can all be set when you enquire.

Timing: weekend dates go first — most families confirm 3–4 weeks ahead. Reach out as soon as you have a likely date.

Payment: a confirmation advance secures your date, with the balance due on the day. Package prices are fixed — nothing is added later.

Add-ons: extra guests beyond your tier, an additional homam, or extended catering are available, priced on request.
